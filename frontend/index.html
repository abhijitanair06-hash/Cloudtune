IDENTIFY / SAVE / DISCOVER

LISTENER

Find a song from the sound around you, or say a lyric you remember. Listener saves the confirmed song to Liked Songs, then lets you add it to a playlist.

01 / SOUND

WHAT'S PLAYING?

Put your device near the music and record a short 10-second sample. Use headphones off if you're trying to identify music playing from another source.

No personal API token is required. Allow microphone access to identify a short music sample.

ABOUT FREE RECOGNITION

Listener uses ShazamIO ↗, an open-source, unofficial integration with Shazam. You do not need an AudD token or paid plan.

This avoids a separate per-user API quota, but Shazam may throttle or change its service; unlimited successful recognition is not guaranteed.

A 10-second recording is sent to the recognition provider only when you press this button.

02 / LYRICS

SPEAK THE LYRICS

Speak or sing a line you remember. Listener transcribes it in your browser and searches a lyrics index. Singing may transcribe less accurately than speaking.

Browser speech recognition availability depends on your browser. Typing lyrics works as a fallback.

Only the words you submit are sent to the lyrics search provider; Listener doesn't save the spoken audio.
